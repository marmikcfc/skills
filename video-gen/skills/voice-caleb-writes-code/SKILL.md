# Voice: Caleb Writes Code

Measured from 10 videos, 16,404 words, 87 min (2025-03 → 2026-07).
Use for technique, not impersonation.

## Thesis

**The diagram is the argument; narration points at it.** The words do not describe
an idea in the abstract — they direct attention to a board that is being built and
never erased.

**The signature: deixis.** `as you can see` runs at **62×** the comparison-corpus
rate, alongside `look at the` and `you can see`. The narration points at something
still on screen. This is inseparable from the visual grammar — see
`explainer-visual-grammar`.

## Moves

- **`And` as default connective.** Over half of all sentences open with a
  conjunction. The effect is one continuous reasoning pass, not a list of points.
- **We-framing for analysis:** the narrator and viewer read the paper together.
- **Point, then explain.** Direct attention first, interpret second.
- **Cut to the real artifact** — the actual paper figure — when authority is needed.
- **Numbers as anchors:** parameter counts, activation percentages, GPU counts.

## Anti-patterns

- Referring to something no longer on screen. The deixis only works with persistence.
- However / Moreover / Furthermore — 0 instances.
- Short staccato sentences; this voice sustains.
- `I` as the dominant pronoun — it's `we`.

## Calibration

- [ ] ≥40% of sentences open with And/So/Now/But
- [ ] Mean sentence length 20–26
- [ ] `we` outnumbers `I`
- [ ] At least one explicit pointing phrase per section
- [ ] Every pointed-at referent is still visible
- [ ] Zero stock transitions
